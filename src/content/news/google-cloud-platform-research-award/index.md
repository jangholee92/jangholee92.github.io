Jangho Lee and Sara Shamekh were awarded a **$20,000 Google Cloud Platform Research Awards Program grant** to support their research.

**Project title:** Diffusion-Based Multi-Sensor Satellite Fusion for Land Surface Temperature Downscaling Across African Cities.

This grant was prepared by **Jangho Lee** and **Sara Shamekh** as co-PIs.
