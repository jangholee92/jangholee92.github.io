This research pillar focuses on the **socio-environmental impacts** of climate change. By merging earth observations with socio-economic datasets, I quantify how extreme heat, flooding, and air quality hazards translate into tangible human and infrastructure risks.

### Key Pillars
*   **Vulnerability & Health Impacts:** Analyzing city-level heat response and predicting temperature-related mortality under various climate and demographic scenarios.
*   **Infrastructure Reliability:** Modeling climate-driven changes in energy demand (e.g., electricity peak demand in Texas) to evaluate grid resilience and future reliability.
*   **Equitable Adaptation:** Investigating the intersection of environmental hazards with socioeconomic inequality, leveraging crowd-sourced data and Google Trends to identify city-level vulnerabilities.
