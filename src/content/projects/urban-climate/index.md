My work in **Urban Climate** aims to understand how urbanization and city form modify local meteorology. I utilize advanced observational techniques and numerical modeling to map neighborhood-scale environmental variations and boundary-layer processes.

### Key Pillars
*   **Dense Sensor Networks:** Deploying and managing high-resolution environmental sensors (e.g., CROCUS Micronet in Chicago) to monitor urban heat islands and flooding in real-time.
*   **Data Quality & Gap-Filling:** Developing automated quality control algorithms and machine learning-based gap-filling methods (e.g., **GA-BiLSTM**) to ensure the reliability of urban environmental data.
*   **Multi-Scale Physical Analysis:** Integrating land surface models (e.g., **ELM**) with satellite and in-situ observations to analyze the interplay between vegetation, impervious surfaces, and the urban atmosphere.
